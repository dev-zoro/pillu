Put your photos in this folder and name them exactly like this:

  photo0.jpg   -> the big heart she clicks at the start
  photo1.jpg   -> heart after the 1st pair of lines
  photo2.jpg   -> heart after the 2nd pair of lines
  photo3.jpg   -> heart after the 3rd pair of lines
  photo4.jpg   -> heart after the 4th pair of lines
  photo5.jpg   -> heart after the 5th (last) pair of lines

Tips
- The picture is cropped to fill the heart, so keep faces near the middle/upper part.
- Wide hearts suit landscape or square photos best.
- Keep each file under ~500 KB so the page loads fast on mobile data.
- Using .png or other names? Change the file names at the top of script.js.
- Until a photo is added, a pink "Photo N" placeholder is shown instead.
